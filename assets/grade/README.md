# Grade Assets

This folder documents grade-level educational materials used for local testing and mission generation.

## Local-only worksheet files

The PDF files under `apps/assets/grade/5th/` are intentionally ignored by Git
and are not distributed with this repository. They remain available in the
author's local workspace for the recorded demo.

To recreate the local setup, obtain the worksheets from the source pages
listed in [References](#references), follow the source provider's terms, and
save them at the documented local paths. The original page and download URLs
are kept below for provenance.

## Grade 5

The local Grade 5 demo worksheets were obtained from K5 Learning's printable
fifth-grade worksheet pages listed below. The PDFs themselves are not part of
the repository.

## Ready-to-copy mission setups

Attach the matching PDF file or files in the Mission Builder before processing the preview. Use one setup per mission.

### Math bundle: place value, powers of ten, and angles

Use this when attaching all three files from `apps/assets/grade/5th/math`.

Mission name:

```text
Grade 5 Math Skills Quest
```

Learning goal:

```text
Students will strengthen Grade 5 math skills across place value, powers of ten, and angle classification by explaining each answer step by step.
```

Teacher prompt:

```text
Use the attached Grade 5 math worksheets as the primary source. Create a short mission that mixes questions about building 5-digit numbers from parts, finding missing factors when multiplying by 10, 100, or 1,000, and classifying angles. Keep the difficulty at Grade 5 level and follow the exercise style from the files. Emily should ask students to explain their reasoning, give step-by-step feedback, and use simple visual language when students make mistakes. Include multiple-choice and next-step questions. Do not introduce concepts that are not represented in the attached worksheets.
```

### Math: build a 5-digit number from the parts

Use with `apps/assets/grade/5th/math/Build a 5-digit number from the parts.pdf`.

Mission name:

```text
Place Value Builder
```

Learning goal:

```text
Students will build and read 5-digit numbers from expanded place-value parts.
```

Teacher prompt:

```text
Use the attached place value worksheet as the primary source. Create a Grade 5 mission where students build 5-digit numbers from thousands, hundreds, tens, and ones. Keep the examples close to the worksheet style. Emily should ask students to identify each place-value part before choosing the final number, then explain any mistake by separating the number into parts.
```

```text
Power of Ten Detective
```

Learning goal:

```text
Students will find missing factors in multiplication equations involving 10, 100, and 1,000.
```

Teacher prompt:

```text
Use the attached multiplying by powers of ten worksheet as the primary source. Create a Grade 5 mission where students solve missing-factor problems involving 10, 100, and 1,000. Angle Sorting Mission
```

### Grammar: future perfect tense

Use with `apps/assets/grade/5th/grammar/grade-5-future-perfect-tense.pdf`.

Recommended class subject: English Language Arts or Grammar.

Mission name:

```text
Future Perfect Time Travelers
```

Learning goal:

```text
Students will recognize and form future perfect tense sentences using "will have" plus a past participle.
```

Teacher prompt:

```text
Use the attached future perfect tense worksheet as the primary source. Create a Grade 5 grammar mission where students identify, complete, and explain future perfect tense sentences. Focus on the structure "will have" plus a past participle and on when the action will be completed in the future. Emily should give short explanations, ask students to justify their choice, and correct tense mistakes without introducing unrelated grammar topics.
```
